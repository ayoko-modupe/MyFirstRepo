Brief Introduction About Myself

Hi, I'm Modupe Ayoko. I’m someone who loves learning, creating, and helping others grow. I’m into tech, fashion, and community development. You can find me teaching students how to build simple tech projects or sewing outfits, I’m always looking for ways to add value. I grew up in Ajegunle, a community where resources were limited, but I found strength in learning and sharing knowledge. I’m passionate about using my skills to inspire and uplift young people in underserved communities.
